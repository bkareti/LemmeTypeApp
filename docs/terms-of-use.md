# LemmeType Terms of Use

*Draft for the maintainer to review before the first App Store submission. It is plain-language text, not legal advice.*

LemmeType is a voice-writing app for iPhone and iPad. By using it you agree to these terms.

## The subscription

- LemmeType is sold as an auto-renewing monthly subscription. The price and the length of any free trial
  are shown in the app, from the App Store, before you subscribe.
- A free trial, when offered, starts when you confirm it in Apple's purchase sheet. You are not charged during the
  trial. If you do not cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, the subscription renews at the price shown and
  your Apple Account is charged.
- The subscription renews automatically each month until you cancel. Payment is charged to your Apple Account.
- You can cancel at any time in Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or from LemmeType's Settings ›
  Subscription › Manage Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for.
- Refunds are handled by Apple, under Apple's rules. Contact Apple through reportaproblem.apple.com.
- A free trial can be used once per Apple Account.

## Using the app

- Dictation in Local mode happens on your device. Cloud speech, when you turn it on, sends the audio of the
  recordings you make with a Cloud mode to LemmeType's server and a speech service. How it is handled is in the
  [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).
- A monthly allowance applies to Cloud speech. When it is used up, Cloud speech pauses until the next billing
  period; Local dictation keeps working.
- Do not use the app to break the law or to record people who have not agreed to be recorded.
- Text produced by speech recognition can be wrong. Check it before you rely on it.
