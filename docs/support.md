# LemmeType help and support

LemmeType turns your voice into text on your device. It understands English.

## iPhone and iPad

1. Open LemmeType and tap **Start Dictating**. Allow the microphone (and, if asked,
   Speech Recognition) the first time.
2. Speak, then tap **Stop**.
3. Check the text, then tap **Copy**. Paste it into any app (touch and hold, then
   **Paste**).

**Dictate in any app (WhatsApp, Messages, Notes…):**

1. Add the LemmeType keyboard: Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New
   Keyboard › LemmeType. It does not need Full Access. In the app you are typing in,
   switch to it with the globe key.
2. Action Button (iPhone 15 Pro and later): Settings › Action Button › Shortcut ›
   Choose a Shortcut › LemmeType › **Dictate Anywhere**.
3. Back Tap (optional): Back Tap lists only your own shortcuts, so make one first. In
   the Shortcuts app, tap **+**, add the **Dictate Anywhere** action from LemmeType, and
   tap **Done**. Then choose it in Settings › Accessibility › Touch › Back Tap ›
   Double Tap (or Triple Tap).

Press once to start and again to stop. You stay in the app you were using, and the text
goes where you were typing. Your iPhone must be unlocked. The other action, **Start
Dictating**, opens LemmeType instead. LemmeType's Settings › Dictate in Any App shows
the same steps.

**Speech engine:** Apple Speech works right away. For a Whisper model, open Settings ›
Models and download one; it stays on your device.

### Subscription

LemmeType Pro is a monthly subscription with a 7-day free trial, bought through the App
Store. Dictation needs an active trial or subscription.

- **Start the trial:** LemmeType › Settings › Subscription.
- **Cancel:** LemmeType › Settings › Subscription › **Manage Subscription** (Apple's
  screen), at least 24 hours before the period ends. You keep it until the end of the
  period you paid for.
- **New phone or reinstalled:** LemmeType › Settings › Subscription › **Restore Purchases**.
- **Refunds** are handled by Apple: <https://reportaproblem.apple.com>.
- **After it ends**, your modes, vocabulary, and settings stay, and you can still open
  Settings, export, and delete your data.

## Mac

Press **⌥ Space** (Option + Space), speak, and press it again. The text appears where
your cursor was. Allow the microphone and Accessibility when asked.

## Common questions

**It says "No speech was detected".** The recording had no speech LemmeType could hear.
Check the microphone and speak a little closer.

**Copied text disappeared.** Copied text is removed after the time set in Settings ›
Keep copied text for. Choose a longer time if you need it.

**Recording stopped by itself.** On iPhone and iPad a recording stops when you leave the
app, take a call, or reach five minutes, so the microphone is never left on. A recording
you start with the Action Button keeps going in other apps until you press it again (or a
call comes in, or five minutes pass). Your audio so far is kept so you can still get the
text.

**Does LemmeType send my voice anywhere?** No. Version 1.0 turns your voice into text on
your device only. See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).

**How do I delete everything?** iPhone and iPad: Settings › Privacy and Data › Delete All Local Data. Mac: Settings › Privacy › Delete All Local Data.
