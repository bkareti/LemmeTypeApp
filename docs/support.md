# LemmeType help and support

LemmeType turns your voice into text on your device. It understands English.

## iPhone and iPad

1. Open LemmeType and tap **Dictate**. Allow the microphone the first time.
2. Speak, then tap **Stop**.
3. Check the text, then tap **Copy**. Paste it into any app (touch and hold, then
   **Paste**).

**Start faster:** add the **Start Dictating** action to the Action Button (iPhone
15 Pro and later: Settings › Action Button › Shortcut) or to Back Tap (Settings ›
Accessibility › Touch › Back Tap). LemmeType's Settings › Start From a Button shows the
steps.

**Speech engine:** Apple Speech works right away. For a Whisper model, open Settings ›
Models and download one; it stays on your device.

## Mac

Press **⌥ Space** (Option + Space), speak, and press it again. The text appears where
your cursor was. Allow the microphone and Accessibility when asked.

## Common questions

**It says "No speech was detected".** The recording had no speech LemmeType could hear.
Check the microphone and speak a little closer.

**Copied text disappeared.** Copied text is removed after the time set in Settings ›
Keep copied text for. Choose a longer time if you need it.

**Recording stopped by itself.** On iPhone and iPad a recording stops when you leave the
app, take a call, or reach five minutes, so the microphone is never left on. Your audio
so far is kept so you can still get the text.

**Does LemmeType send my voice anywhere?** No. See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).

**How do I delete everything?** iPhone and iPad: Settings › Privacy and Data › Delete All Local Data. Mac: Settings › Privacy › Delete All Local Data.
